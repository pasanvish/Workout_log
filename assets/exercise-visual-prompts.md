# Exercise visual artwork prompts

The anatomy illustration and four additional exercise guides were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The three sheets were exported as WebP assets for the app. The two exercise sheets reference the CC0 Romanian deadlift image from [Open Exercise Illustrations](https://github.com/hitmanrepo/open-exercise-illustrations/tree/2786aefa4b36a08faeca8114e0d1eb0af3ef6732).

## Front and back anatomy

```text
Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: exercise app anatomical reference sheet. Create one elegant realistic 3D sports anatomy illustration with the SAME fictional adult athletic male model twice, face and physique identical, front orthographic and back orthographic. Adult with medium warm-brown skin tone, short cropped dark hair, clean shaven, normal athletic proportions, wears only dark graphite mid-thigh fitted performance shorts; barefoot. Smooth human skin with realistic subtle muscular definition and softly shaded visible details of shoulders, pecs, arms, forearms, abs, lats, calves. Clinical but appealing athletic anatomy, editorial sports-science textbook style. EXACT TWO views side by side in TWO equal columns, straight front on left, straight back on right, with no perspective, perfectly level eyes and feet, figures the same height from top of head to tips of toes, hands down slightly out from body, fingers straight relaxed, legs slightly apart symmetrical pose. Large full body and the entire figure inside each half, tiny equal vertical margin with heads at approx 5% and feet at approx 95%. View widths separated by generous gap. Even diffuse softbox lighting against uniform very dark charcoal (#131e27). Wide portrait figures in a square canvas. No text, no watermark, no arrows, no labels, no displayed highlighted muscles, no numbers, no borders. The shorts cover groin and buttocks.
```

## One-arm kettlebell swing

```text
Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: one-arm kettlebell swing instructional image pair. The image is a style reference only. Keep exactly the reference's same pale-gray, faceless, anatomically proportional adult male exercise mannequin, dark graphite shorts, bare feet, slate black studio with subtle blue rim light, photorealistic human musculature and muted charcoal gym equipment. Create one WIDE 2-column reference sheet with TWO equally sized panels of the same figure performing two different phases of a one-arm kettlebell swing. Both panels show full head-to-toe body and kettlebell in the identical locked three-quarter side profile camera facing right, with the same ground line and same stance at shoulder width. Panel one LEFT: hip hinge, hips back and knees mildly bent, straight neutral back, right hand single-handed with kettle bell swinging back BETWEEN the legs close to the upper inner thighs, free arm trails back naturally, weight clearly behind knees, shoulder connected to the extended arm. Panel two RIGHT: fully tall upright stance after hip drive, whole arm straight forward at about chest height with right hand grasping ONE dark kettlebell handle, free arm hanging loosely behind torso. Exactly one kettlebell per panel. Dark uniform background separate the panels with open negative space. Empty space at the edges and at the bottom. No lettering, symbols, labels, logos, borders, gloves or jewelry. Do not use a bent-over barbell or show a squat. Human form and exercise identification matter more than ornament.
```

## Dumbbell row and mobility views

```text
Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: four exercise pose views for a fitness app. Reference is only for athlete and charcoal studio style. Use exactly the same faceless pale-gray adult male exercise model, charcoal training shorts, bare feet, subtle blue rim lights, human proportions and joint structure, charcoal flooring. Render one wide artwork subdivided into EXACTLY FOUR equal 2x2 cells, with large black gaps, artwork carefully centered in each cell, entire head and feet each within the cell. Top-left and top-right are the same camera and viewpoint on an athlete performing a TWO-DUMBBELL BENT OVER ROW, side/three-quarter profile facing right. Hips hinged, hips and knees stationary through exercise, torso inclined almost parallel to floor, neutral neck. TOP LEFT beginning: both arms pointing down from shoulders, one separate dark handled dumbbell firmly held in each hand, wrists under shoulders, no barbell. TOP RIGHT finish: shoulders stay in the same position, elbows draw backwards alongside ribs, both compact dark dumbbells level near sides of lower ribs. BOTTOM LEFT separate static SUPINE HAMSTRING STRETCH: athlete lying flat face up on floor, left leg flat, right leg up near vertical with knee gently extended, both hands holding the back of right thigh, head down. BOTTOM RIGHT separate static KNEELING WRIST STRETCH: athlete takes an all-fours posture kneeling on both knees, both palms flat on the floor and fingertips clearly pointing back toward own knees, elbows straight. Clinical sports reference, no arrows, no typography, no visible panels or lines, just dark negative space between exactly four views.
```
